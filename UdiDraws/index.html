Gesture Guide

👋 Hover / Idle

Hand detected but no drawing gesture active. A cursor tracker follows your index fingertip.

☝️ Draw Mode

Extend only your Index Finger (fold middle, ring & pinky). Draws continuous strokes on canvas.

✌️ Select Mode

Extend Index + Middle fingers (peace sign). Hover over toolbar items to pick colors, tools, or actions.

✊ Erase Mode — Closed Fist

Curl all fingers into a closed fist (index, middle, ring & pinky folded). Instantly erases canvas strokes in mid-air!

🧹 Air Erase — 3 Fingers

Extend Index + Middle + Ring fingers to erase a wide area. Acts as a brush eraser pass over the canvas.

💡 Tips
  • Keep hand ~1–2 ft from camera
  • Good lighting improves tracking accuracy
  • Move slowly for smoother lines
  • Use ✌️ to hover over toolbar buttons
  • Use ✊ closed fist to erase in air
  • Use 3 fingers (Index + Middle + Ring) for wide erasing
  • You can also click toolbar items with your mouse
  • Switch cameras from the header dropdown
